# NORCOM Server Rules
1. **Survival (Easy).** Difficulty is Easy; play fair and have fun. Death still costs time and gear.
2. **No land claims / no homes / no TPA / no RTP.** Explore, build, and agree with friends in chat. Waystones and Universal Graves are the travel/recovery tools.
3. **One coin economy.** NORCOM Coins (Simple Eco) are used for shops, auctions, player trades, quest rewards, and NPC traders. Do not expect separate currencies.
4. **No X-ray / cheating.** Fair play only.
5. **Be decent.** No harassment. Admins may mute/kick/ban.
6. **Backups exist, but do not rely on rollbacks for carelessness.**
7. **Optional Physics Mod Pro** is allowed if you buy it yourself (Patreon). It is never shipped in this pack.
